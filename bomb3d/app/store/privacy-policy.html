Bomb 3D Simulator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Bomb 3D Simulator is a game for kids and families. We take privacy seriously, so the app is built to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, levels, pets, skins, settings and the username you type) is saved only on your own device so you can keep playing. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet

The app works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Children

Because the app collects no information at all, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Contact the developer through the support link on the App Store page.
